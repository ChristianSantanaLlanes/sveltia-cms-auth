Benchmark access

tesla.com cannot be reached from this build sandbox: Tesla's edge (Akamai) blocks the datacenter IP for direct requests and for every public screenshot service tried, web.archive.org is blocked by this environment's egress policy, and Chromium here has no outbound HTTPS at all. Live side-by-side capture of the real funnel is therefore impossible from inside this session. Critics score against docs/reference-spec.md — the Tesla funnel written down as measurable properties — plus their own knowledge of that funnel, and every round must still name the single largest remaining gap.
